Positions against the league

Every team fields every position for a full season: 640 PA at catcher and 700 at each other spot, DH included. Our lineup optimizer decides who plays where on each team to get the most batting WAR. It allows for fatigue (hitters do a little worse the more they play) and for how a fielder's defense changes when he moves position, measured from Statcast. Time no rostered player fills better than a replacement-level player goes to one, at 0 WAR. No rostered hitter gets more PA than our learned playing-time model expects of him, so time he is expected to miss goes to his backups, as in the forecast. Pitching is our staff optimizer's share of each team's innings, the same as the forecast uses, with each pitcher's innings and rate from our learned models. Swingmen (at least a tenth of their games started) can pitch in the rotation or the bullpen, and each pitcher is counted in the one he throws most of his innings in.

What would move the number

The lineup is 4.0 WAR below the league average. The biggest gaps are second base (−1.4), designated hitter (−0.7), third base (−0.6), left field (−0.5) and first base (−0.4).

The rotation is concentrated: Paul Skenes, Jared Jones and Braxton Ashcraft make up 11.3 of the 22.3 pitching WAR, so a long absence from any of them would cost a large part of the staff's value.
