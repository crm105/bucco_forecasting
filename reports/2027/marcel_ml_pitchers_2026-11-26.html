How much to trust this

This forecast comes from the Marcel with learned pitchers (our own player projections, pitcher innings and rates from learned models, with lineups and pitching staffs optimized) model. Each model is tested by forecasting every MLB team's record for every season it can cover, using only information from before that season, and the scores below come from that test. Models that use only team records cover every season from 2001 to 2026; models built from player projections cover only the seasons with projection files or rosters (ZiPS from 2011, Steamer from 2012, our own from 2009), so their scores rest on fewer, more recent seasons. The 2020 season (60 games) is left out of the scores.
