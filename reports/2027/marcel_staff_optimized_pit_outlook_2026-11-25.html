Positions against the league

Every team fields every position for a full season: 640 PA at catcher and 700 at each other spot, DH included. Our lineup optimizer decides who plays where on each team to get the most batting WAR. It allows for fatigue (hitters do a little worse the more they play) and for how a fielder's defense changes when he moves position, measured from Statcast. Time no rostered player fills better than a replacement-level player goes to one, at 0 WAR. Pitching is our staff optimizer's share of each team's innings, the same as the forecast uses. Swingmen (at least a tenth of their games started) can pitch in the rotation or the bullpen, and each pitcher is counted in the one he throws most of his innings in.
