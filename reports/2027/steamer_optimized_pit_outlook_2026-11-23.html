Positions against the league

Every team fields every position for a full season: 640 PA at catcher and 700 at each other spot, DH included. Our lineup optimizer decides who plays where on each team to get the most batting WAR. It allows for fatigue (hitters do a little worse the more they play) and for how a fielder's defense changes when he moves position, measured from Statcast. Time no rostered player fills better than a replacement-level player goes to one, at 0 WAR. Pitching is the projection as given; the rotation is pitchers who start at least half their games.

What would move the number

The lineup is 4.4 WAR below the league average. The biggest gaps are third base (−1.0), first base (−0.9), left field (−0.8), second base (−0.5) and catcher (−0.4).

The rotation is concentrated: Paul Skenes, Braxton Ashcraft and Jared Jones make up 11.8 of the 19.1 pitching WAR, so a long absence from any of them would cost a large part of the staff's value.
